6. AUDIT QUESTIONS & FINAL CONCLUSIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Is the 100% TEST result internally consistent?
    --> YES. All 1,635 test predictions match their corresponding ground truth labels with high softmax confidence (mean = 0.9999, median = 1.0000, min = 0.9428). Confusion matrix, F1-scores, ROC curves, and probability distributions show zero mathematical contradictions.

Q2: Are all four classes being predicted?
    --> YES. All four distinct classes are actively predicted in exact proportion to their ground truth distribution (Healthy_Leaf: 152, Healthy_Nut: 472, Mahali_Koleroga: 641, yellow leaf disease: 370). There is zero class collapse or bias toward majority classes.

Q3: Is there any obvious class/prediction distribution anomaly?
    --> NO. Predicted class distribution matches the actual test class distribution with 0 delta across all classes.

Q4: Is there any obvious train-vs-test distribution anomaly?
    --> NO. The class proportions between cleaned TRAIN (6,109 images) and untouched TEST (1,635 images) are closely aligned: Healthy_Leaf (9.53% train vs 9.30% test), Healthy_Nut (28.99% train vs 28.87% test), Mahali_Koleroga (37.93% train vs 39.21% test), yellow leaf disease (23.56% train vs 22.63% test).

Q5: Is there any obvious visual dataset shortcut?
    --> NO evidence of artificial shortcuts detected. Both TRAIN and TEST exhibit natural agricultural photography conditions with varied lighting, leaf/nut orientations, backgrounds, and natural diseased lesions. There are no synthetic watermarks, borders, or class-specific resolution signatures.

Q6: Is there evidence from this analysis that the TEST result is invalid?
    --> NO. Step 8/9 verified 0 exact duplicates and 0 near-duplicate leakage between TRAIN and TEST. The model operates as a high-capacity ResNet-50 feature extractor with strong inter-class visual separability.

7. PROJECT LIMITATIONS TO REPORT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * 1. Domain Specificity: The dataset was acquired under specific field camera conditions. Performance in dramatically different lighting or unseen geographic orchards should be evaluated with external field data.
  * 2. High Confidence Distribution: The softmax probabilities are heavily concentrated near 1.0, typical of over-parameterized deep neural networks trained with fine-tuned cross-entropy loss.
  * 3. Closed-Set Assumption: The model operates under a closed-world 4-class assumption and assumes test inputs belong strictly to one of the four categories.
  * 4. Leaf vs. Nut Disparity: While Leaf classes and Nut classes have distinct anatomical features, intra-organ disease differentiation (e.g. Healthy_Leaf vs yellow leaf disease) relies on color/texture degradation, which the model captured cleanly.
